Turn the phone sideways to play.

  • In a level: the arrow keys move; Space or Enter selects or works a platform; P pauses; Q retries; Esc leaves.
  • In the menus: the function keys shown on screen, or the arrow keys and Enter.
  • Maps (F2): the arrow keys, PgUp/PgDn, Home and End choose a map; Enter or Space plays it; Tab or P opens the password screen; L lists the level sets (the free maps, the original's once it is added, and level packs imported with the saves); Esc goes back.
  • Statistics (F8): the arrow keys and PgUp/PgDn turn the pages; Tab or S sorts; Esc goes back.
  • Options (F5): F1 enhanced mode, F2 setup (images and music from the original game once it is added, and the view), F3 full background, F4 flat stone, F5 sound.
  • Gamepad: the D-pad or the left stick are the arrows, A Enter, X Space, B Esc, Start P, Y L, Back (held) Q, LB and RB PgUp and PgDn.
  • Touch: tap a line of a menu, or a map; the buttons beside the picture are the keys. On Android the first tap goes fullscreen; on an iPhone, add the page to the home screen.

Scores, settings and the original game added here stay in this browser. ?original after the address plays one session without the enhancements, ?free one without the original's files.
